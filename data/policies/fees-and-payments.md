# Fees and Payments

## Consultation fees
Fernleaf Clinic charges a flat consultation fee per specialty. Orthopaedics consultations with Dr. Meera Iyer cost 900 rupees. Cardiology consultations with Dr. Arjun Rao cost 1,200 rupees. General Medicine consultations with Dr. Kavya Menon cost 600 rupees. The fee covers the doctor's time for one visit, including examination and a written prescription or plan. Investigations such as X-rays, ECGs and blood tests are billed separately at the rates listed in each department's policy.

## Free follow-up within 7 days
A follow-up visit with the same doctor within 7 days of the original consultation is free of charge. The follow-up must be for the same complaint. Patients should mention at the time of booking that it is a follow-up so that the receptionist can mark it correctly. After 7 days, the regular consultation fee applies again.

## Accepted payment methods
We accept UPI (all major apps), debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), and cash. Payment is collected at the front desk after the consultation, not at booking. We do not accept cheques. For card payments above 2,000 rupees, please carry the physical card, as tap-to-pay limits may apply.

## Taxes and receipts
GST is not applicable to medical consultations, so the fee you are quoted is the fee you pay. Every payment receives a printed receipt with the clinic's registration number and the doctor's registration number, which can be used for insurance reimbursement claims. Receipts can also be sent by SMS or email on request.

## Prepayment rules
Most patients do not need to pay in advance. Patients who have missed two booked appointments without notice are asked to prepay the consultation fee at booking, as described in the cancellation and rescheduling policy.
