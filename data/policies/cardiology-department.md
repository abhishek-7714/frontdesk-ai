# Cardiology Department

## About the department
The Cardiology department is led by Dr. Arjun Rao, who focuses on preventive cardiology and hypertension care. The consultation fee is 1,200 rupees. The department sees patients for blood pressure management, cholesterol, palpitations, routine heart check-ups and follow-up after hospital treatment.

## ECG on site
A resting ECG is available on site and costs 350 rupees. It takes about 10 minutes and the doctor reviews it during the same consultation. No preparation is needed, though loose clothing makes it easier.

## Echocardiogram
Echocardiography (echo) is available by appointment only, on selected weekday mornings. The doctor will advise if an echo is needed after the consultation, and the front desk will book it separately.

## What to bring
Please bring your blood pressure log if you measure at home, ideally readings from the past two weeks, morning and evening. Bring previous ECGs, echo reports, blood test results and any hospital discharge summaries, plus a list of all regular medicines with doses.

## Urgent symptoms
Fernleaf is an outpatient clinic. Chest pain, severe breathlessness, fainting, or a racing heartbeat with dizziness need emergency care. Do not wait for a cardiology appointment: call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department immediately.

## Questions
For any question about cardiology department that is not covered here, call the Fernleaf front desk during clinic hours, Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM. The receptionist can answer most questions on the call, and anything that needs a doctor or a staff member is passed on with a call-back the same day.
