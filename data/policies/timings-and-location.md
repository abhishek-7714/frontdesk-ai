# Timings and Location

## Clinic hours
Fernleaf Clinic is open Monday to Saturday in two sessions: a morning session from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM and an evening session from 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM. Appointments are offered in 30-minute slots within these sessions. The clinic is closed on Sundays. The last appointment of each session starts 30 minutes before the session ends.

## Sundays and public holidays
The clinic is closed on Sundays. On national public holidays the clinic usually runs only the morning session; the receptionist will confirm holiday timings when you call. In an emergency on a Sunday or holiday, please call 112 or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

## Address
Fernleaf Clinic, Road No. 12, Banjara Hills, Hyderabad, Telangana. The clinic is on the ground and first floors of the building, close to the Road No. 12 main junction. Lifts are available, and the building is wheelchair accessible.

## Contacting the front desk
The front desk phone line is 040 0000 4321 (+91 40 0000 4321), answered during clinic hours, Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM. Outside these hours, calls are answered by the AI receptionist, who can book appointments and take messages for the team. WhatsApp messages to +91 90000 04321 are read during clinic hours and are the quickest way to share a report or a photo of a prescription. For anything that is not urgent, email frontdesk@fernleafclinic.example; the team replies within one working day. Doctors' messages about delays or leave reach the front desk through the clinic's internal system, and the front desk then informs affected patients by phone or WhatsApp.

## Parking and getting here
Free parking is available in the basement of the building for patients during clinic hours. Two-wheelers park in the marked area near the ramp. Auto-rickshaws and cabs can drop patients at the main entrance. If you are using a cab app, search for Fernleaf Clinic, Banjara Hills.

## Arriving for your appointment
Please arrive 10 minutes before your appointment time to complete registration. Doctors try to see patients on time, but a delay of up to 15 minutes can happen when an earlier patient needs extra time. The front desk will keep you informed of any longer delay.
