# Test Preparation

## Fasting tests
A lipid profile (cholesterol test) and a fasting blood sugar test require fasting for 10 to 12 hours before the sample is taken. The easiest way is to have an early dinner, fast overnight and come in the morning session. Plain water is allowed and encouraged during the fast. Do not drink tea, coffee, juice or milk.

## Medicines before tests
Continue your regular medicines unless the doctor has told you otherwise. If you take diabetes medicines or insulin, ask the doctor how to adjust them on a fasting day, because skipping food while taking these medicines can cause low sugar.

## Non-fasting tests
Most other blood tests, such as a complete blood count, thyroid tests and HbA1c, do not need fasting. Urine tests are best done with the first sample of the morning, using the sterile container provided at the clinic.

## Sample collection timings
Blood samples are collected from 10:00 AM to 12:30 PM and from 4:00 PM to 6:30 PM. Fasting samples should be given in the morning session. Most routine reports are ready the same evening or the next day and are shared by SMS and email.

## X-ray and ECG
No special preparation is needed for an X-ray or an ECG. Please remove metal jewellery from the area being X-rayed, and wear loose clothing for an ECG.

## Questions
For any question about test preparation that is not covered here, call the Fernleaf front desk during clinic hours, Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM. The receptionist can answer most questions on the call, and anything that needs a doctor or a staff member is passed on with a call-back the same day.
