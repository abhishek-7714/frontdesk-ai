# General Medicine

## About the department
The General Medicine department is led by Dr. Kavya Menon, who practises family medicine and sees patients with fevers, infections, routine check-ups and long-term conditions such as diabetes and thyroid problems. The consultation fee is 600 rupees.

## Walk-in hours
General Medicine accepts walk-in patients between 10:00 AM and 11:00 AM, Monday to Saturday, on a first-come, first-served basis. Outside that hour, General Medicine is by appointment only. Booked patients are always seen before walk-ins.

## Vaccinations
We offer adult vaccinations including influenza, tetanus, hepatitis B, typhoid and pneumococcal vaccines. Vaccines are given after a short consultation. Vaccine charges are separate from the consultation fee and depend on the vaccine brand available.

## Health check-ups
Routine health check-ups include a consultation, blood pressure and weight measurement, and a set of blood tests chosen by the doctor. Some blood tests need fasting; please read the test preparation policy before your visit.

## Children
General Medicine at Fernleaf sees patients aged 12 and above. For younger children, we recommend a paediatrician; the front desk can share nearby options.

## Questions
For any question about general medicine that is not covered here, call the Fernleaf front desk during clinic hours, Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM. The receptionist can answer most questions on the call, and anything that needs a doctor or a staff member is passed on with a call-back the same day.
