# Cancellation and Rescheduling

## Free rescheduling
You can reschedule or cancel an appointment free of charge up to 2 hours before the appointment time. Call the clinic and the receptionist will offer the next available slots with the same doctor or, if you prefer, with another doctor in the same department.

## Late changes
Changes made less than 2 hours before the appointment are treated as a late cancellation. We understand emergencies happen, and a single late cancellation has no penalty. Please still call so that the slot can be offered to another patient.

## No-shows
A no-show is a booked appointment where the patient neither attends nor calls to cancel. After two no-shows, the patient is asked to prepay the consultation fee when booking future appointments. Prepayment can be made by UPI over the phone link sent by the front desk. The prepayment requirement is removed after three consecutive attended visits.

## Running late
If you are running late, please call. Patients arriving more than 15 minutes late may be asked to wait for the next free slot or to reschedule, so that patients who arrived on time are not delayed.

## Doctor-initiated changes
Occasionally a doctor may need to reschedule because of a surgery or emergency. In that case the clinic will call you as early as possible, offer priority slots, and waive any prepayment already made by moving it to the new appointment.

## Questions
For any question about cancellation and rescheduling that is not covered here, call the Fernleaf front desk during clinic hours, Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM. The receptionist can answer most questions on the call, and anything that needs a doctor or a staff member is passed on with a call-back the same day.
