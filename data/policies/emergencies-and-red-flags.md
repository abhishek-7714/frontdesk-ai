# Emergencies and Red Flags

## Fernleaf is an outpatient clinic
Fernleaf Clinic is an outpatient clinic. We do not have an emergency department, intensive care or ambulances. We cannot treat medical emergencies on site, and the receptionist cannot give medical advice over the phone.

## Red-flag symptoms
The following symptoms need emergency care immediately: chest pain or pressure, difficulty breathing or severe breathlessness, fainting or loss of consciousness, signs of stroke such as a drooping face, weak arm or slurred speech, severe bleeding that does not stop, a severe head injury, or thoughts of self-harm.

## What to do in an emergency
If you or someone with you has any red-flag symptom, call 112 now or go to the nearest hospital emergency department immediately. Do not wait for a clinic appointment and do not drive yourself if you feel faint. If someone is unconscious and not breathing, start CPR if trained and call 112.

## What the receptionist will do
If a caller mentions a red-flag symptom, the receptionist stops the booking, tells the caller to call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department, and alerts the clinic's staff so that a nurse can follow up later.

## Urgent but not emergency
For problems that are urgent but not dangerous, such as a high fever for three days or a painful sprain, the receptionist will try to offer the earliest available slot and alert staff so they can call back if a sooner slot opens.

## Questions
For any question about emergencies and red flags that is not covered here, call the Fernleaf front desk during clinic hours, Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM. The receptionist can answer most questions on the call, and anything that needs a doctor or a staff member is passed on with a call-back the same day.
