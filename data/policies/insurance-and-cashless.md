# Insurance and Cashless Treatment

## Empanelled insurers
Fernleaf Clinic is empanelled with Star Health, HDFC ERGO, ICICI Lombard and Niva Bupa. Empanelment means we can share documentation with these insurers directly and that our invoices meet their claim requirements. We are not currently empanelled with other insurers, but patients insured elsewhere can still claim reimbursement using our receipts.

## Outpatient consultations are reimbursement only
Outpatient consultations and investigations at Fernleaf are not cashless. Patients pay at the clinic and then claim reimbursement from their insurer, if their policy covers outpatient care. Many retail health policies do not cover outpatient consultations, so please check your policy terms. The front desk cannot confirm coverage on behalf of your insurer.

## What to carry
Please carry your insurance policy card or e-card and a government photo ID to every visit. The front desk will note your policy number on the receipt. For reimbursement, insurers typically need the itemised receipt, the doctor's prescription and any test reports, all of which we provide.

## Corporate health plans
Some employers have outpatient health plans that reimburse consultations. Patients on corporate plans should follow the same process: pay at the clinic, collect the receipt and prescription, and submit them through your employer's claim portal.

## Help with claims
If an insurer asks for additional documents, such as a doctor's certificate or a duplicate receipt, contact the front desk. Duplicate receipts are free. Medical certificates are issued by the treating doctor at their discretion.

## Questions
For any question about insurance and cashless treatment that is not covered here, call the Fernleaf front desk during clinic hours, Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM. The receptionist can answer most questions on the call, and anything that needs a doctor or a staff member is passed on with a call-back the same day.
