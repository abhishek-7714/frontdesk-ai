# Orthopaedics Department

## About the department
The Orthopaedics department is led by Dr. Meera Iyer, who has 14 years of practice in joint, knee and sports injuries. The consultation fee is 900 rupees. Appointments are 30 minutes long, which allows time for a proper examination and discussion.

## Conditions we see
Common reasons for an orthopaedic visit include knee pain, back pain, neck and shoulder stiffness, sprains, sports injuries, ligament injuries such as ACL tears, fracture follow-ups, arthritis and joint pain, and post-surgery reviews. Patients with sudden severe injuries, an obviously deformed limb or an open wound should go to an emergency department instead.

## What to bring
Please bring any previous X-rays, MRI or CT scans and their reports, both films and CDs if you have them. Bring prescriptions from previous doctors and a list of medicines you take regularly, including pain relievers. If you have had surgery on the affected joint, bring the discharge summary.

## What to wear
For knee and leg examinations, please wear loose clothing such as track pants or shorts so the doctor can examine the joint easily. For shoulder examinations, a loose T-shirt is best. Changing gowns are available if needed.

## X-ray on site
Digital X-ray is available on site during clinic hours. A single-view X-ray costs 450 rupees. The doctor will request it during the consultation if needed, and results are usually ready within 20 minutes, so they can be reviewed in the same visit. MRI is not available on site; we refer to partner imaging centres nearby.
