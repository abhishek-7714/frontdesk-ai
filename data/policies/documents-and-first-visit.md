# Documents and Your First Visit

## Arrive early
Please arrive 10 minutes before your appointment on your first visit. Registration takes about five minutes at the front desk and creates your Fernleaf patient record, which is used for all future visits.

## What to bring
Bring a government photo ID such as Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence or passport. Bring previous prescriptions, test reports and scans related to your problem, and a written list of all medicines you take, with doses. If you have insurance, bring your policy card or e-card.

## Your intake
When you book by phone, our receptionist may ask two or three short questions about your problem, how long it has been going on and which medicines you take. These answers are shared with your doctor before the visit so that the consultation can start quickly. You can add or correct anything when you arrive.

## Privacy
Your information is shared only with the clinic's doctors and staff involved in your care. We do not share patient information with employers or insurers without your consent, except where required by law.

## Bringing someone with you
You are welcome to bring a family member or friend into the consultation. For patients who need help with language, a family member can translate; our front desk staff speak English, Hindi, Telugu and Tamil.

## Questions
For any question about documents and your first visit that is not covered here, call the Fernleaf front desk during clinic hours, Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM. The receptionist can answer most questions on the call, and anything that needs a doctor or a staff member is passed on with a call-back the same day.
